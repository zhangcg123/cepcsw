## Outcome counts and state survey

Using ordinary non-catastrophic definitions produced 154 nominal no-change
events, 35 partial recoveries, 257 good recoveries, 18 moderate overshoots, and
57 moderate degradations. Three representatives from each class were rerun
with complete component dumps.

The nominal no-change class mixes different physics outcomes. Events 15/2 and
407/4 have 3.65% and 5.60% owned loss but LCIO is already within 0.33% and
0.09% of truth; identity selection is therefore correct, not missed recovery.
Event 37/7 has 7.54% loss and remains near -7.72%; this is a genuine missed
recovery, with the identity lineage dominant through hit 0.

Good recoveries 369/1, 225/5, and 203/3 switch from the outer low-pT state to
near-truth radiative states at hits 5, 5, and 7. Partial recoveries 420/3,
243/0, and 452/4 switch at hits 6--7 but select insufficient-loss children.
Moderate overshoots 65/6 and 102/4 switch at hit 5 to excessive-loss children.
Event 469/6 stays on identity through hit 1 and overshoots only at hit 0, where
the winner has weight 0.343 versus identity weight 0.260; this is a useful
near-boundary selection case rather than an extreme tail.

Low-loss moderate degradations 14/4 and 413/3 switch away from a strongly
dominant identity at hit 5; 286/8 switches at hit 4 and the identity is absent
after reduction. These events connect the light-category optimization to the
previous clean-like reverse over-selection mechanism.

## Interpretation

The survey does not support optimizing a single generic "light failure." The
actionable classes are:

1. genuine missed recovery, where identity remains dominant despite a biased
   outer state;
2. partial recovery, where the correct transition region is found but the loss
   child is too weak;
3. moderate overshoot, where an excessive-loss child wins, often at hits 5--0;
4. low-loss false correction, where identity should remain selected.

The next quantitative comparison should use paired winner/truth-compatible
innovation likelihood and prior odds at the decisive hit for representatives
of all four classes. Category labels and owned loss alone cannot define the
desired branch because some tracks are already truth-compatible before reverse
radiative correction.
