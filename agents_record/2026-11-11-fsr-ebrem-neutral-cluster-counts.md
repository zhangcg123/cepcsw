| Generator FSR | 1800 | 472 | 240 | 88 |

Counts are PHOTONS, not distinct clusters. One photon shower can contribute
to charged and neutral clusters, or fragment into several neutral clusters.
Any positive PFA MC-labelled contribution gives the loose presence count;
the >50% criterion requires one neutral cluster to be dominated by that one
photon and its saved descendants. This is a separate reconstructed object,
not an angular-isolation requirement or a true deposited-energy fraction.
Neutral means a charge-zero PFO without tracks; cluster energy must be finite
and positive and it must contain hits. Window membership uses the exact
saved collection indices for the owning electron, not a new angular match.

## Truth identity and cluster association

FSR uses the saved generator-origin status1, generator photon MC index and
final stable electron owner. Material photons require creator subtype3 and
the exact emitting selected-electron G4track ID. An eBrem MC root is accepted
only with a unique exact stored birth momentum(float32 representation),
birth vertex(double), and direct selected-electron MC parent. Candidate
collisions or absent roots remain unclassified; no nearest-neighbour guess.

Both roots follow saved MC descendants, including conversions/showers. Native
PFA debug MC weights are attributed by the existing exact float32
PDG/momentum/endpoint identity procedure; ambiguous ancestry contributions
are withheld. Positive cluster contributions are normalized by all MC weights
in that cluster for the dominance test. Pre-cluster/PFO identity comes from
the earlier validated snapshot gate:928events reproduce original cluster
energies/positions, complete hit multisets and PFO charge/track ownership;
72remain unresolved. Current original REC sizes/mtimes exactly match all20
archived validations. Native debug MC labels match current REC MC arrays.
